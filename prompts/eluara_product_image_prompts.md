Use a premium, minimalist fashion e-commerce aesthetic inspired by the clean product photography and understated styling of Everlane and American Tall, without copying any exact existing product or campaign. Photorealistic, refined, natural-looking materials and accurate garment construction. No text, logos, watermarks, borders, collages, or props that distract from the garment. Use a consistent adult model and styling within each product's two images.
